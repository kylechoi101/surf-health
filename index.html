Research roadmap

What is live, and what is still research.

Shorelife’s production forecast is narrow on purpose. Only fecal indicator bacteria risk is live today. Other cards show published backtests or planned work.

Active

Fecal indicator bacteria

The live daily forecast estimates enterococcus exceedance risk from official California sample history and coastal and weather inputs.

Published

Weather signal without lab history

Published 2026-10-15. A backtest used six days of coastal weather with no lab history or beach identity. Weather carried real day-to-day signal, mostly from rain. Serving is unchanged.

Planned

Additional bacterial indicators

Planned. We may expand beyond marine enterococcus to freshwater E. coli, fecal coliform, and ddPCR source-tracking markers as supplementary public risk signals.

Shorelife provides daily water quality forecasts for California beaches. We use official bacteria samples, coastal conditions, and model calibrations. A Shorelife forecast is a model estimate. It is not an official county lab test.
