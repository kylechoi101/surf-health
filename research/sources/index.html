Where the data
comes from.

Shorelife uses eight primary data sources. All are public or government-archived. The pipeline runs daily at 9 AM PT. County-direct scrapers cover 12 of 15 California coastal counties. They surface official postings within hours of county publication. The statewide data.ca.gov export is frozen at 2026-03-05 and publishes no current status. Each source is attributed in the model feature space and versioned in the parquet archive.

github.com/kylechoi101/surf-health/blob/main/backend/scripts/fetch_county_advisories.py ↗

Live advisory and closure postings from each county health department public source. San Francisco uses the data.sfgov.org Socrata API. Eight original counties plus Humboldt and Sonoma use regex parsers on static HTML. San Luis Obispo uses Playwright-rendered LLM extraction on its ArcGIS dashboard. When a county scraper succeeds, its records are authoritative for that county. Stale state-feed records auto-demote. This rollout alone cleared 12 zombie postings older than a year. Closures are exempt from the 14-day acute window so chronic situations stay visible until officially lifted.

County-Direct Sample Feed
Update cadence
Daily, alongside the advisory scrape
github.com/kylechoi101/surf-health/blob/main/backend/scripts/fetch_county_advisories.py ↗

Same-day numeric water-quality values from each county public source, parallel to the BeachWatch historical archive. Fields include enterococcus, fecal coliform, E. coli, and total coliform. We apply AB411 single-sample thresholds (ENT ≥104, FECAL ≥400 MPN/100mL) to the latest reading per station. We do not wait for the state advisory feed to catch up. We use this feed today for cross-validation. It can become a near-real-time training signal later. We currently capture 200 sample readings. LLM extractions require sample_date within 90 days, analyte on a strict whitelist, and a plausible value.

Fields used
analyte (ENTEROCOCCUS/FECAL_COLIFORM/E_COLI/TOTAL_COLIFORM)value (MPN/100mL)sample_dateexceeds_limitstation_codesource_url

Update cadence
Historical archive only — the data.ca.gov export is frozen at 2026-03-05 and publishes no current results.
data.ca.gov/dataset/beach-monitoring ↗

Official marine enterococcus culture-based sample results and the statewide advisory feed. The multi-year archive sets each beach rating and trains the ML challenger. We also use this feed as an advisory fallback in three counties where direct scraping is blocked or unavailable. Monterey blocks datacenter IPs. Santa Barbara lists sampling sites but does not publish current status. Mendocino and Santa Cruz have no first-class scraper yet. The data.ca.gov export is frozen. Its newest sample is dated 2026-03-05, so it supplies historical training data only. Recent results arrive from the live BeachWatch database (beachwatch.waterboards.ca.gov, added July 2026) and CEDEN/SafeToSwim. County-direct scrapers override advisory status for the 12 covered counties.

Reanalysis archive of hourly precipitation, cloud cover, shortwave radiation, UV index, and near-surface wind at 0.1° resolution. The 72 hours of rain before each morning update feed the served rating. Other fields feed the ML challenger solar inactivation and wind plume features.

§ 02
Pipeline

Forecast-safe cutoff at 5 AM PT.

The pipeline runs daily at 9 AM PT. All environmental inputs stop at 5 AM PT on the forecast day. Same-morning lab results do not enter the features. Open-Meteo data is cached per (lat, lon, date) in a local parquet store and updated each day. A full 6-year backfill runs only on the first cold-cache CI run.
