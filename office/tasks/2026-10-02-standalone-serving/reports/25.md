Added `lookup_serving.build_candidates(curated_dir, forecast_date, *, training_window_days=1095, min_recency_days=30)`. It reproduces training's served beach set without importing `training.py` (which pulls in xgboost and torch). The rule has four parts:
- Read `beach_day.parquet`. Drop `support_status == "unsupported"` rows, rows with no `sample_time` or `enterococcus_value`, and rows with an implausible time (before 2000-01-01, or later than now + 2 days).
- Keep the `training_window_days` ending at the newest `sample_date` in the file. The daily workflow passes 1095.
- Use history strictly before `forecast_date`. Keep beaches with at least 3 history rows whose newest sample (by `sample_time`) is no older than `min_recency_days`. The workflow passes 30. `None` disables the cutoff.
- Row order is first appearance in the history, as in training.

The output carries only the columns derivable from the rule: `beach_id`, `forecast_date`, `latest_sample_date`, `sample_age_days`, `sample_recency_band`, `forecast_label_mode`, `is_beta_forecast`. The environmental display columns and `forecast_generated_at` are Phase 50.
