## Done

Implemented the `forecast` subcommand in `backend/scripts/florida_prototype.py` and unit tests in `backend/tests/test_florida_prototype.py`.

Key achievements:
- `forecast`: Filters stations for forecast date D requiring ≥3 samples in the 365 days before D and newest sample within 30 days of D (223 stations for 2026-10-05).
- Model selection: Checked `backtest.json` metrics where `logistic` outperformed `logistic_free_phase` (AUROC 0.744 vs 0.743), dynamically choosing the served `logistic` form (`R, RF, W, S`).
- Zero leakage: Strictly trains on sample-days before D and extracts features using only samples before D.
- Open-Meteo forecast rain: Fetches hourly precipitation via `https://api.open-meteo.com/v1/forecast` (`past_days=3`, `timezone="America/New_York"`), caches to `data/raw/florida/rain_forecast_<D>/`, and computes precipitation in the 72 h ending 05:00 America/New_York on D.
- Persistence floor: Floored exceedance probability `p_exceed` to at least 0.20 whenever the most recent prior sample exceeded (`last_exceeds == True`).
- Risk bands: Assigned risk bands according to defined cutpoints (Low < 0.20, Moderate 0.20–0.30, High 0.30–0.70, Very High ≥ 0.70).
- Deliverables: Wrote `data/experiments/florida/forecast_2026-10-05.parquet` and `forecast_2026-10-05.csv` containing all 13 required columns for 223 stations.
- Unit tests: Added tests verifying `apply_persistence_floor`, `assign_risk_band` cutpoints, and no leakage on or after D using synthetic fixtures without network calls.
