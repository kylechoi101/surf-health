## Done

Implemented the `backtest` subcommand in `backend/scripts/florida_prototype.py` and added a no-leakage unit test to `backend/tests/test_florida_prototype.py`.

Key achievements:
- `backtest`: Reused pure functions from `backend/scripts/multistate_feasibility.py` (`lab_features`, `fit_offset_logit`, `predict_offset_logit`, `metrics`, `cluster_bootstrap`, `rain_72h_to_5am`, `fetch_rain`, `_coord_key`).
- Rain caching: Fetched missing coordinates for Florida (`27.9_-80.5` and `30.3_-87.5`) through 2026-10-05 into `data/raw/openmeteo_multistate/`.
- Walk-forward evaluation: Refit monthly models on all prior eligible sample days (with a 365-day history requirement) and scored the 12 calendar months 2025-10 through 2026-09 across 241 Florida stations (7,943 scored predictions).
- Models evaluated: Lookup baseline, persistence, `logistic` (features R, R·F, W, S), and `logistic_free_phase` (features R, R·F, W, S, sin(season)).
- Metrics & Bootstrap: Computed AUROC, AUCPR, Brier score, and within-station AUROC for each model, refit coefficients per month, and beach-cluster bootstrap 95% confidence intervals on delta vs. lookup. Saved results to `data/experiments/florida/backtest.json` and predictions to `data/experiments/florida/FL_usf_walkforward_predictions.parquet`.
- Unit tests: Added `test_no_leakage_on_or_after_target_date` verifying that observations on or after target date D do not leak into features or predictions for D.
